# Installing modularOS

modularOS is in private beta. People on the beta get the installer and the full guide with their invitation.
This page says what you will need, so you can check before you ask for access.

## What you need

- A Mac with Apple Silicon and macOS 14 or later. A MacBook with a notch is the best fit; on a Mac without a
  notch the widget sits at the top center of the screen.
- An administrator account on that Mac.
- Claude Code (with a paid Claude plan) or Codex. With Codex alone the widget and the agent list work; Glauka,
  notes and advisors need Claude Code.
- About 20 GB of free disk space and 20 to 40 minutes for the first install. The app is built from source on
  your Mac.

## How to get access

Ask for access on [moduleone.ai/os](https://moduleone.ai/os). We invite people one by one.

The public install guide will replace this page with the public release.
